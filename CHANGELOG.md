# Change log

What changed in each version of OMS Pick, newest first. The app updates itself, so you land on the
newest one whether you read this or not.

## Unreleased

Built, not yet in a release you can download.

- **Sign in with Instagram and Facebook.** One button instead of twenty minutes of making your own
  Meta app. It is waiting on Meta's App Review, so the button is there but does nothing until they
  clear it. The own-app route still works and always will.
- **Pick the Page from a list.** No more typing a Page id by hand. The list comes from the permissions
  you ticked on the consent screen.
- **Every count of photographs opens those photographs.** A number on screen is now a thing you can
  press, including the one the copy check reports.

## 1.0.0, 18 September 2026

The first build a person other than me can run.

- Signed and notarised by Apple, so it opens without a warning and can update itself from then on.
- Reads a folder with a vision model on your own Mac and writes a caption, tags, subjects, the setting
  and the mood for every photograph.
- Type a sentence and the grid narrows to the photographs that match. Build it lays them out in the
  order of the day.
- Writes the caption in your own voice from your last hundred captions, using your own Claude or
  DeepSeek key. It sends words, never pictures.
- Posts carousels to Instagram and a Facebook Page, now or on a schedule from the menu bar.
- Plan a week of posts from one sentence, then check every one before anything goes out.
- A photograph that has been posted is left alone for 90 days.
- Every AI feature can be switched off. It still works as a plain picker and poster.
